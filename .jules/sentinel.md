## 2026-11-12 - Enforce Fail-Closed Temporal Validation in Capability Dispatch and Historical Verification
**Vulnerability:** In `CapabilityRegistry.consume_token_and_record_dispatch` and `AuthorizationAuthority.verify_historical_capability` (`epistemicos/authorization.py`), execution timestamps (`started_at`, `exec_time`) were evaluated against capability expiration timestamps (`expires_at`) using unvalidated direct float inequalities (`float(started_at) > float(expires_at)`).
